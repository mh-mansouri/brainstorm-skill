Plan or brainstorm the topic.

1. Think broadly first: list several real options, including ones that are not obvious.
2. Pick the best one with a one-line reason: most effective for the least effort.
3. Give the steps to do it, short and in order, and name every step that needs the user's OK.

Rules:
- Show ideas as a table, sorted by priority (most useful first).
- Before suggesting an idea that may already be done, check the live page or profile.
- Plan only. You may write the plan into planning files, but do not post, schedule, send, render, deploy or edit code in this turn. End the turn with the plan; execution happens in a later turn on the normal model.
- Keep the turn short and focused. If the planning model is unavailable or its limit is reached, plan on the normal model and say so in one line.
